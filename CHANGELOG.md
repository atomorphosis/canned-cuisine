# Changelog

## 0.3.0-beta.1

- Added the playable Pressure Canner with atomic batch processing, automation, preview, and persistent inventory.
- Added procedural canned meals with nutrition, effects, naming, appearance, returned cans, and native composition tooltips.
- Added reloadable ingredient profiles, archetypes, and effect rules with bundled Vanilla and optional culinary-mod data.
- Added optional JEI, EMI, Jade, AppleSkin, Tooltip Overhaul, and KubeJS integrations.
- Combined the Pressure Canner preview bars into one complete result tooltip and localized objective atlas requirements.
- Added curated discovery loot, advancements, English and Spanish localization, and development data diagnostics.
- Hardened canonical composition validation, persistent codecs, atlas synchronization, and integrated-server data cleanup.
- Added minimal and full compatibility builds to continuous integration.
- Consolidated culinary matching into six clear canned preparations: Stew, Mushroom Soup, Porridge, Compote, Vegetable Medley, and Field Ration.
- Replaced the legacy quality score with independent categorical preparations, additive food values, affinity-funded effects, and effect-derived Vanilla rarity.
- Prevented zero-food catalysts from creating nutrition or saturation through pressure processing.
- Hid optional effects from JEI and EMI when their providing mod is absent.
- Added formula-specific label variation so visually similar non-stackable meals remain distinguishable.
- Added Rock Salt as an affinity-neutral duration enhancer found as an occasional extra drop from ores.
